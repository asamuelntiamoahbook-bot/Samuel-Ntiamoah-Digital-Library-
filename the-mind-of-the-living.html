About the Book

The Mind of the Living turns attention inward, inviting readers to reflect on the mind and the role it plays in how we understand ourselves and experience life.

It belongs to Samuel Ntiamoah's wider body of reflective and inspirational writing, exploring the inner questions, choices and perspectives that shape human becoming.

This is The Mind of the Living, a distinct title in the author's collection.
