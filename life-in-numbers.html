LIFE IN NUMBERS

By Samuel Ntiamoah

A reading introduction

Life Doesn't Have a Number, but Life Is in Numbers

Life in Numbers uses numbers as metaphors for the experiences, decisions and turning points that shape human life.

A number can be more than a symbol for counting. It can become a way to reflect on beginnings, identity, adaptation, roots, decisions, protection and the moments when a person must choose a direction.

About This Book

Through short inspirational reflections, the book invites readers to look at familiar numbers from a different perspective and consider the lessons that everyday life may be communicating.
