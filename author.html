About the Author

A writer shaped by observation, reflection and the journey of becoming.

Samuel Ntiamoah is a Ghanaian author and writer whose work explores human relationships, identity, personal growth, time and the experiences that shape everyday life. His writing is driven by a continuing interest in how people understand themselves, relate with others and give meaning to the different seasons of their lives.

His journey as an author began with a decision to take his ideas seriously and keep developing them. From early manuscripts to published books, the process has taught him the value of patience, discipline, revision and the courage to keep working on an idea.

Rather than treating writing as a collection of conclusions, Samuel approaches it as an invitation to think. His books raise questions, offer frameworks and encourage readers to examine familiar experiences from fresh perspectives.

The Author’s Journey

Samuel’s writing journey has been shaped by ideas, setbacks, learning and the decision to keep creating. What began as personal writing gradually became a commitment to publish and share his work with readers.

2016 · The beginningAn early manuscript takes shape

Behind the Hours of Darkness marked an important beginning in his journey as a published-minded writer. The first manuscript was modest, but it strengthened his determination to keep writing.

2017 · Learning through experienceTaking books to readers

With Ant of Life, Samuel continued exploring ways to share his work. Early book-launch experiences taught him that publishing also requires patience, planning and persistence.

The continuing journeyBuilding a platform for his books

He continued revising, producing and presenting his work, while developing a digital library to make his books and ideas more accessible to readers.

This journey is still unfolding. Each book represents another opportunity to explore an idea, learn from experience and invite readers into a thoughtful conversation.

My Writing Philosophy

I believe writing should do more than communicate information; it should invite people to see, question and understand life differently. A meaningful piece of writing does not simply tell readers what to think. It gives them room to reflect, recognise something within themselves and discover a new way of looking at the world.

My work is rooted in the journey of becoming. I am interested in the picture a person carries within, the experiences that shape identity, the choices that influence direction and the relationships through which we encounter one another. I write about familiar things—time, love, connection, truth, discipline, identity and everyday life—with the hope of uncovering the deeper questions often hidden inside them.

I also believe that ideas must move beyond beautiful words. Truth must be lived, understanding must go deeper than communication, and personal growth must find expression in the choices we make. For this reason, I often use reflections, questions, images and simple frameworks to help readers examine their own experiences.

My writing is reflective rather than prescriptive. I do not assume that every reader will arrive at the same conclusion. Instead, I hope each book becomes a thoughtful companion: one that challenges familiar assumptions, opens new perspectives and encourages readers to take responsibility for their own journey.

The Ideas Behind My Books

Every book begins with an idea worth exploring. Although my books approach life from different directions, they share a common interest in understanding people, recognising possibilities and reflecting on the choices that shape our experiences.

The Power of Human Attraction

Explores human connection and the nature of relationships. Through the images of the flower, stone and tree, it invites readers to reflect on different relationship identities and the ways people connect.

The Woman of You

Offers an encouraging reflection on a woman’s identity, value and journey of becoming—looking beyond outside expectations to the person she is discovering and developing within.

Life in Numbers

Uses numbers as simple but meaningful pictures of life. Each number becomes an opportunity to consider beginnings, potential, choices, growth and the different seasons of our journey.

Behind the Hours of Darkness

An early work in my writing journey, reflecting the beginnings of my commitment to put thoughts into words and share them with readers.

Ant of Life

A work from an important early stage of my publishing journey, representing the continued effort to develop ideas and bring them before readers.

The Mind of the Living

Reflects on life, the inner self, truth and the personal battles and experiences that influence how we understand our journey.

The Battle of If

Explores the struggles, uncertainties and choices that form part of everyday life, and the lessons that can help us move forward and become who we are meant to be.

These books are different conversations, but they return to one continuing question: how can we understand life more deeply and live with greater awareness of who we are becoming?

“I write to give thoughts a picture, experiences a voice and readers an invitation to discover what they may not have noticed within themselves.”
“A book begins as a thought. It becomes meaningful when its ideas enter another person's life and give them something worth considering.”

Samuel continues to write and develop projects from Accra, Ghana. Through his digital library, he hopes to make his work easier to discover and bring readers closer to the ideas behind each book.
