About the Book

The Battle of If explores the questions, possibilities and inner contests that accompany the journey of life. It reflects on the choices we face, the lessons we gather and the person we are becoming through our experiences.

It invites readers to look beyond the uncertainty of “if” and consider how today's decisions and responses shape tomorrow's story.

A reflective work about meeting life's questions with awareness, courage and purpose.
