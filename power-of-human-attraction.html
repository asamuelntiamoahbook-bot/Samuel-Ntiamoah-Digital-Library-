Reading introduction

Welcome to the Reading Preview

The Power of Human Attraction explores human relationships, connection, understanding and the experiences that draw people towards one another.

Human relationships are not built on feelings alone. They involve awareness, understanding and the willingness to appreciate the realities of one another. The book invites readers to reflect on the different ways people connect and relate.
