The Author

Samuel Ntiamoah

Samuel Ntiamoah is a Ghanaian author and writer whose work explores human identity, relationships, personal growth and the realities of becoming.

His writings draw inspiration from everyday human experiences, observations and the deeper questions that shape our existence. Through his work, he invites readers to understand themselves, recognize the value of their experiences and appreciate the connections they build with others.

“Understanding life begins with understanding ourselves.”

Samuel Ntiamoah
