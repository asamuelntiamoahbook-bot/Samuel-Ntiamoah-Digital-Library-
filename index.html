About the Author

Samuel Ntiamoah

Samuel Ntiamoah is a Ghanaian author, thinker, and writer whose work explores human identity, relationships, personal growth, and the realities of becoming.

His writings are inspired by everyday human experiences, observations, reflections, and the deeper questions that shape our existence.

Through his work, Samuel encourages readers to understand themselves, recognize the value of their experiences, and appreciate the connections they build with others.

His philosophy is rooted in a simple belief: understanding life begins with understanding ourselves.
