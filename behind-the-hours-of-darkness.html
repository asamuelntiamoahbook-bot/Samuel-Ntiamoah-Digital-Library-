About the Book

Behind the Hours of Darkness reflects on the difficult experiences that can disturb our lives and raise deep questions about suffering, its causes and its purposes.

Through a Christian perspective, the book encourages readers not to allow painful seasons to become the final definition of their lives. It points toward faith, understanding and the hope that can be found in Christ.

A reminder that a season of darkness does not have to be the end of the story.
